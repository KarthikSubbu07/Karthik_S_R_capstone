# Work From Home Policy

## Eligibility
All employees are eligible to work from home up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval and team requirements.

## Expectations
While working remotely, employees are expected to:
- Be available during core hours (10:00-15:00 local time)
- Attend all scheduled meetings via video call
- Maintain responsiveness on Slack and email
- Use the company VPN at all times

## Home Office Equipment
A one-time home office equipment allowance of £200 per year is available. This can be used for monitors, ergonomic chairs, or keyboards.

Co-working space costs are NOT reimbursable under this policy.

## Returning to Office
Employees may return to the office at any time during designated WFH days. No advance notice is required for unscheduled office visits.

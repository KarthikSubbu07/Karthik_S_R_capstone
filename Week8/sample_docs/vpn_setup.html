VPN Setup Guide

Getting Started

All employees working remotely must use the company VPN. Setup instructions are available on the IT portal.

Password Reset

Self-service VPN password reset is available through the IT portal. The reset takes effect immediately after confirmation.

If you are locked out of your account, contact the IT helpdesk on extension 4040 or via email at it-helpdesk@acme.example.com. Helpdesk hours are 08:00-18:00 weekdays.

Common Issues

  • Connection drops: try reconnecting; if persistent, contact IT
  • Slow speeds: check your home connection first
  • Authentication failures: verify your password reset was completed

# Parental Leave Handbook

## Overview
This handbook details the parental leave entitlements and process for all employees of ACME. It applies to maternity, paternity, adoption, and shared parental leave.

## Eligibility
All employees with 12 months or more of continuous service are eligible for full parental leave. Employees with less service receive statutory leave only.

## Duration
- Maternity leave: 26 weeks paid + 26 weeks unpaid (52 total)
- Paternity leave: 2 weeks paid + 12 weeks paid parental leave
- Adoption leave: matches maternity entitlements
- Shared parental leave: up to 50 weeks shared between parents

## Application
Apply through the HR portal at least 15 weeks before expected leave start. Late applications may receive reduced pay.

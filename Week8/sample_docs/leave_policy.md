# Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
All full-time employees are entitled to 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. This is in addition to public holidays observed in your location.

Annual leave does not carry over to the next calendar year. Please plan your time accordingly.

## Sick Leave
Sick leave is granted up to 10 working days per year. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate is required. Contact the HR coordinator Priya Sharma at priya.sharma@acme.example.com or on +44 20 7946 0123 for queries.

## Parental Leave
New parents are entitled to 12 weeks of paid parental leave, regardless of gender. Apply at least 4 weeks in advance through the HR portal.

## How to Apply
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance. Manager approval is required. For escalations, contact David Okafor (david.okafor@acme.example.com).
